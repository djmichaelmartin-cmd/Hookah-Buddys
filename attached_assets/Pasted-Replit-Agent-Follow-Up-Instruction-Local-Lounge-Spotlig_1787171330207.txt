Feature Goal

On The Lounge feed, the “Lounge Spotlight” section should only show businesses/lounges that are relevant to the logged-in user’s city and within a 25-mile radius.

Every day, show 3 businesses with the highest ratings within that local radius.

When the user clicks anywhere inside the Lounge Spotlight widget, redirect them to:

`/lounges`

On the `/lounges` page, those same 3 spotlight businesses should appear at the top of the page before the rest of the lounge/business listings.

Location Logic

Use the logged-in user’s saved profile location as the default location source.

Use this priority:

1. User profile latitude/longitude, if available
2. User profile city/state, geocoded to latitude/longitude
3. User-selected city/location, if the platform has a city selector
4. If no location is available, show a prompt: “Add your city to see local Lounge Spotlights near you.”

The radius should be:

`25 miles`

Only show businesses within 25 miles of the user’s location.

Business Eligibility

The Lounge Spotlight should use businesses that are appropriate for the Lounge/business discovery experience.

Eligible businesses should include business profiles that are:

* Active
* Publicly visible
* Not suspended
* Not hidden
* Not rejected
* Approved by admin if business approval is required
* Within 25 miles of the user’s profile city/location
* Have ratings/recommendations enabled
* Have at least one rating/recommendation, if ratings exist
* Are marked as eligible for Lounge Spotlight

Add or use this field:

`eligibleForLoungeSpotlight: boolean`

Default:

`true`

Admin should be able to turn this off for any business.

Do not limit business creation by business type. However, Lounge Spotlight should only show businesses that are locally relevant and suitable for the Lounge discovery area.

Recommended eligibility logic:

A business can appear in Lounge Spotlight if:

* `eligibleForLoungeSpotlight = true`
* `status = active`
* `visibility = public`
* business has valid city/state or latitude/longitude
* business is within 25 miles of logged-in user
* business is not flagged, suspended, or banned

Rating Logic

Rank eligible businesses by:

1. Highest average rating
2. Highest number of reviews/recommendations
3. Verified business status
4. Featured/sponsored status, only as a tie-breaker
5. Shortest distance from user

Use this ordering:

* `averageRating DESC`
* `reviewCount DESC`
* `isVerified DESC`
* `isFeatured DESC`
* `distance ASC`

Minimum recommendation:

If possible, require at least 3 reviews before a business can rank above businesses with fewer reviews.

Example:

A 4.8 rating with 40 reviews should rank above a 5.0 rating with 1 review.

Use a weighted rating formula if possible:

`weightedRating = (averageRating * reviewCount + 4.0 * 5) / (reviewCount + 5)`

Then sort by:

* `weightedRating DESC`
* `reviewCount DESC`
* `distance ASC`

Daily Rotation

Every day, calculate the top 3 Lounge Spotlight businesses for each user location/city.

The same logged-in user should see the same 3 businesses throughout the day.

The list should update once per day.

Use the user’s timezone if available.

If timezone is not available, use server date or UTC date.

Daily rotation behavior:

* Calculate all eligible businesses within 25 miles.
* Rank by weighted rating.
* Select the top 3.
* Rotate the order daily so the same business is not always shown first.

Example:

Day 1:

1. Business A
2. Business B
3. Business C

Day 2:

1. Business B
2. Business C
3. Business A

Day 3:

1. Business C
2. Business A
3. Business B

Do not show random businesses that are poorly rated just for rotation. The 3 businesses should come from the highest-rated local businesses.
