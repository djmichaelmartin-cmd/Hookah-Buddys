Replit Agent Follow-Up Instruction: Add Facebook-Style Business Page Features

Do not rebuild the platform. Only expand the existing Business Profile feature set.

Business Profiles should work similarly to Facebook Business Pages, while keeping the HookahDolls brand, privacy rules, moderation rules, and member-profile requirement.

Important existing rule:

A user must have a personal/member profile before creating a Business Profile.

Do not limit the type of business. Business owners should be able to create a profile for any legitimate business, brand, service, organization, local company, creator business, or professional service.

Business Profiles should feel like public business pages users can follow, message, review, check into, browse, and engage with.

Add the following Facebook-style business page features:

* Follow button

Business owners should be able to choose one main CTA button:

* Message
* Call Now

* Book Now
* Get Quote
* Shop Now
* Order Now
* Reserve
* Buy Tickets
* Sign Up
* Learn More
* View Menu
* View Services
* Request Appointment
* Contact Us

Only show CTA buttons that the business has enough information to support.

Examples:

* Do not show “Call Now” unless phone number exists.
* Do not show “Visit Website” unless website URL exists.
* Do not show “Book Now” unless appointments are enabled.
* Do not show “Shop Now” unless products/catalog are enabled.

Important Rules

Do not rebuild.

Do not remove the existing personal/member profiles.

Do not remove Doll/Bro profiles.

Do not remove credits, memberships, pricing, Doll monetization, messaging, verification, or The Lounge feed.

Do not limit business type.

Do not allow anonymous business profiles.

Do not publicly expose business owner private profile information.

Do not show payment data, verification documents, government ID, or private messages publicly.

Keep business messaging separate from paid Doll messaging.

Business pages should be public-facing pages connected to a real member profile.

Design Direction

Use HookahDolls styling, not Facebook branding.

Design should feel:

* Social-media familiar
* Business-friendly
* Clean
* Premium
* Dark lounge themed
* Mobile responsive

Use:

* Blue for social actions: Follow, Message, Book, Contact
* Gold for premium actions: Promote, Verify, Feature, Sponsor
* Green for open/active/confirmed
* Red only for alerts, reports, violations, and destructive actions

Final Goal

The finished Business Profile system should feel like Facebook Business Pages inside HookahDolls:

A member can create a business page, add business info, post updates, upload photos/reels, list services/products, create events/offers, receive messages, collect reviews, get check-ins, view insights, manage team access, verify the business, and promote the page.
