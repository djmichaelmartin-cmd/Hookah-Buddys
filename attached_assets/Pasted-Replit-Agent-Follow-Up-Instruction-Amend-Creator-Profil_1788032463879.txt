6. Submit Button

Button:

“Send Custom Request”

Custom Request Rules

Implement these rules:

1. Customer and Creator Mutual Reuse Agreement

The customer and Creator must mutually agree whether or not the Creator can reuse or utilize customer-paid custom content for promotional use.

Do not allow reuse permission unless both agree.

2. Customer Provides Request Details

The customer must provide requested photo/video details to the Creator.

Request details are required before submission.

3. Creator Cannot Reuse Without Permission

Creator cannot use customer-paid custom content for promotional use, public posts, albums, Sparks, previews, advertising, marketing, or any other public purpose without customer permission.

4. Online Creator Response Time

Online Creators must respond to custom photo/video requests in a timely manner.

Required response time:

Within 2 hours of receiving the request.

5. Requests Only to Online Creators

Customers can only send Custom Photo/Video Requests to Creators who are actively online.

If Creator goes offline before request is submitted, block submission and show:

“This Creator is no longer online. Please try again when they are available.”

6. No Credit Capture Without Delivery

Creators cannot accept customer credits without custom photo/video delivery within 2 hours of accepting the request.

Use credit escrow/hold logic:

* When customer submits request, place credits on hold.
* Do not release credits to Creator immediately.
* Creator must accept the request.
* Creator must deliver the custom photo/video within 2 hours after accepting.
* Credits are released only after delivery is sent.
* If Creator fails to deliver within 2 hours after accepting, automatically refund or release the held credits back to customer.
* If Creator declines the request, release credits back to customer.
* If Creator does not respond within 2 hours, expire request and release credits back to customer.
